01 / THE RESCUE GAP

The first search is often the most dangerous.

Collapsed structures, unstable debris and narrow access routes can place rescuers at risk before a victim has even been located. RADARHEX is designed as an early-entry sensing prototype: the robot approaches the unsafe area, checks for presence and structural movement, examines route clearance, and sends the evidence to a command screen outside the hazard zone.

The system does not claim to identify a confirmed victim. It helps an operator find a presence candidate, understand why an alert was raised, and decide where a trained rescue team should verify next.

MISSION PRINCIPLESend a sensor first. Send a rescuer with evidence.

Reduce unnecessary exposure while keeping the final rescue decision with a trained human operator.

FIELD CHALLENGEVisibility can be zero.

Radar presence and vibration evidence remain useful when cameras are blocked by darkness, dust or debris.

OPERATOR NEEDEvidence must be understandable.

The dashboard explains live sensor states, range, route obstruction and the rule that produced each rescue priority.

03 / WORKING PROTOTYPE

Built from real sensors. Explained with honest rules.

The current build connects the field hardware to a live browser dashboard. Each component has a clear responsibility and every major warning can be traced back to the sensor evidence that produced it.

TECHNICAL HONESTYDecision support—not automatic victim confirmation.

RADARHEX reports a candidate that requires human verification. Bearing, GPS and validated confidence are not displayed unless supported by future hardware and field tests.

PROJECT BRIEF / 2026

RADARHEX

RADARHEX is a search-and-rescue sensor-pod prototype. This build combines C4001 presence telemetry, an analog vibration sensor, and a servo-directed ultrasonic obstacle scanner. Radar detections remain range-only because the present C4001 does not provide a bearing. A transparent rule engine combines available signals into an operator assessment without claiming victim identity.
